phase deliberately **relocates** content across sibling files (a ref split, A5/A6) and into two new,
independently unit-tested scripts (`loop-budget.sh`, `resolve-ai-workflow-mode.sh`) whose own branch

guaranteed to mismatch by design whenever content moves out of (or into) that file. The
reconciliation is done at the **combined** level — every outcome traced from its base location to
exactly one live destination, with no unaccounted loss:

- **`STOP`/`blocked` prose lines — preserved or increased.** `loop.md` base: 12 lines. `loop.md` +
