## Preventions

- When a guard's false branch has materially different meanings for different sub-cases, split it
  (add an `elif`) and make at least the dangerous sub-case loud.
- For best-effort external-state mutations, re-read the target state after a failed write before
  declaring failure — a flaky read or concurrent actor may already have reached the goal state.
